Can I test the App? +

Yes! We encourage anyone interested in Emerge24 to use the free test we offer in app! Showing you how our rapid systems work! Simply select the test mode, select your preffered "panic" test button and our system does the rest.

Can I use Emerge24 away from home? +

Yes! Unlike traditional home security systems, Emerge24 is fully mobile. It connects to your smartphone's live GPS location—meaning whether you are commuting, traveling between Cape Town and Johannesburg, or out for a run, protection follows you wherever you go across 2,000+ suburbs nationwide.

What type of assistance does Emerge24 provide? +

Private security, Medical EMS support, Fire and rescue, and Stand By Me.

Why is Emerge24 specifically beneficial? +

Emerge24 facilitates access to over 330 independent private security companies and over 60 Medical EMS providers. Why is this beneficial? This system allows our clients to get the best and most rapid support across South Africa on one mobile app.

How does Stand By Me work? +

“Stand By Me” is an assistance feature. A stand-by service for our clients. Stuck on the highway late at night? Security Stands with you until safety is confirmed by you. It offers support in vulnerable situations.

How do i activate my account? +

Account activation takes 2 mins in app. Once you select your plan, you proceed to a checkout page. Once you have purchased your plan you are automatically activated
